§ The Crisis

The Problem Is Not
Access — It's Interpretation

Government departments already receive court judgments through systems like CCMS. The documents are accessible. But each judgment is 20–40 pages of dense legal prose. Officers must manually read every page, interpret complex phrasing, and sometimes calculate deadlines like "within 8 weeks from the date of pronouncement." This is where the system breaks down — not at access, but at comprehension and action.

§ The Solution Pipeline

From Judgment Received
to Action Defined

Step 01

I

PDF Intake

Judgment PDFs are ingested from CCMS or uploaded directly. OCR handles scanned documents.

→

Step 02

II

AI Extraction

NLP models extract actions, deadlines, departments, and obligations from dense legal text.

→

Step 03

III

Confidence Scoring

Each extracted item is assigned a confidence score. Low-confidence items are flagged for review.

→

Step 04

IV

Human Verification

Officers verify AI outputs before they become official. The human is always in the loop.

→

Step 05

V

Action Dashboard

Verified actions appear on a live dashboard with deadlines, owners, and compliance status.

§ Key Features

Designed for Real
Government Workflows

Critical
CR

Contempt Risk Indicator

Visual risk levels based on deadline proximity. Red, amber, green indicators help officers prioritize the most urgent compliance actions first.

EL

Evidence-Linked Extraction

Every extracted directive links back to the exact sentence in the PDF. Officers verify instantly without re-reading 40 pages of dense legal text.

DS

Decision Summary Cards

Judgments broken into clean, scannable cards showing action, responsible department, deadline, and current compliance status at a glance.

BL

Bilingual Support

Full support for English and Kannada, making the system usable at the district level. Hindi support planned for national-scale adoption across states.

VE

Voice-Based Explanation

Officers can listen to a short audio explanation of the judgment in simple language — enabling quick understanding without reading the full document.

New
TL

Timeline View

A visual flow from judgment to action to deadline. Track progress, see the current state, and identify bottlenecks in the compliance pipeline.

§ The Distinction

Not Summarization —
Structured Extraction

Most existing approaches focus on summarizing documents. That still leaves the officer with interpretation work. Our approach is fundamentally different — we focus on structured extraction, verification, and tracking. NyayaLens is designed as a system that fits into real workflows, not just a tool that generates text.

  • ✓ Every output is verifiable — linked to the exact source text in the judgment PDF
  • ✓ Every decision is traceable — full audit trail from extraction to verification to action
  • ✓ Human review is always part of the loop — AI assists, humans decide
  • ✓ Integrates with existing systems like CCMS without requiring major infrastructure changes
  • ✓ Scalable from state-level to national-level through eCourts integration

§ The Vision
